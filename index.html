About

Hi, I'm Bryan.

Studio LN is my design studio. I'm a San Diego designer and marketer who builds brands, campaigns, websites and the tools that keep them running, for music retailers, authors, artists, nonprofits and events.

By day I'm the Events Coordinator at San Diego Music Studio, a NAMM Top 100 music store, where I run the social media, the Shopify website and the event promotion. I also handle digital marketing and e-commerce for Reeds For Less, the studio's sister reed shop: I built its Shopify store, shoot its product photos and run its email marketing.

I started Studio Ln as a freelance studio in 2022, designing book covers and launch kits for author Armando F. Sanchez. Since then the work has grown into author websites, certificate-of-authenticity systems for artists, wayfinding icons and the templates and builders in this shop.

I hold a Business Administration degree in Management from San Diego State University's Fowler College of Business, with a minor in LGBTQ+ Studies.
